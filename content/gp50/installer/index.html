Firmware installer

  • Unofficial firmware for the Valeton GP-50
  • Build the modified firmware in your browser using your official GP-50 V1.1.0 file.
  • Your file stays on your computer.

1. Release

    2. Your stock firmware

    • Download the official GP-50 V1.1.0 firmware from Valeton and extract the .bin file.

    Downloading sends the release version to Supabase to count download attempts. Your firmware file stays on your computer.

    3. Install with Valeton Suite

    1. Connect the pedal, open Valeton Suite and select the downloaded file in Firmware Update.
    2. Keep the pedal connected until the update finishes.
    3. After the update, check the pedal’s Version screen. For v0.1.0, it should show A1.1.0i.

    If the GP-50 freezes or won’t start, see troubleshooting and recovery.
